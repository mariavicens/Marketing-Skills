Grade 3 · Number Sense
Is 347 closer to 300 or 400? How do you know?
"I think about the number line… 347 is only 47 away from 300 but 53 away from 400, so it's closer to 300."
Grade 7 · Ratios & Proportions
A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of sugar. How much sugar for 9 cups of flour?
"If 3 → 2, then 9 is 3×3, so sugar = 3×2 = 6 cups."
